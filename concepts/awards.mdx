**Awards** are funding you've won, tied to the projects and programs they pay for. Ulysses tracks each award's lifecycle, including obligations, drawdowns, reimbursements, and clawback warnings.

Awards can be added by hand or from a sync.

## Where to find it

Open **Awards** in the [web app](/interfaces/web-app).
