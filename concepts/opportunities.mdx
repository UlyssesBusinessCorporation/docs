---
title: "Opportunities"
description: "Federal and state funding opportunities, scored against your projects and programs."
---

**Opportunities** are federal and state Notices of Funding Opportunity (NOFOs). Ulysses scores opportunities against your projects and programs and stores the matches. Each match has a score and the reasons behind it.

## Where to find it

Open **Opportunities** in the [web app](/interfaces/web-app), or search NOFOs in **Discover**.
