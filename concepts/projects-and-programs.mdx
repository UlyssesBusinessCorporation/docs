title: "Projects and programs"
description: "The capital work and public services you track, and how each one is funded."
---

**Projects and programs** are the capital work and public services you track. A project is capital work, such as a bridge reconstruction. A program is an ongoing service, such as homelessness services.

Each one has a department, budget, dates, links, funding status, and a capital stack: the mix of funding sources paying for it. Projects and programs can also carry federal, state, and CIP identifiers.

## Where to find it

Open **Projects** in the [web app](/interfaces/web-app), then open one to see its details and funding status.
