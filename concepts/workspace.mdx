Your **workspace** is your agency's account in Ulysses, and the boundary for its data and access. It holds your team, user permissions, and department-level access. Everything you see belongs to the active workspace.

## How it fits together

Projects and programs are the work and services that get funded. Opportunities are funding you could pursue for that work, and awards are funding you've won. Connections and datasets bring in data from outside Ulysses. Everything lives in your workspace.

## Joining and switching

Join your agency's workspace with an invitation, or sign up to create your own personal workspace. If your email is associated with more than one workspace, you can switch between them in the product.

What you can see depends on your role in the active workspace.

## Where to find it

Open **Workspace** in the [web app](/interfaces/web-app) to manage your team, user permissions, and department-level access.
