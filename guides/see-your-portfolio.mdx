Keep your full portfolio of capital projects and ongoing programs in a single view.

Open a project to see its department, budget, dates, funding status, and capital stack.

## Where to start

- Open **Projects** in the [web app](/interfaces/web-app).
- Read how projects and programs work in [Projects and programs](/concepts/projects-and-programs).
- Add one that's missing with [Create a project or program](/guides/create-a-project).
