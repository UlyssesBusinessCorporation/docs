## How Ulysses works

In the product, your **workspace** (tenant) holds the organizations and records your team can access.

- **Projects** — capital projects and programs you track
- **Opportunities** — funding opportunities you can match and pursue
- **Awards** — funded awards tied back to the work they support
- **Activity** — recent changes across the portfolio

Use the [web app](https://app.tryulysses.com) for day-to-day work, or the [CLI](/cli/install) for scripts and agent workflows. Both talk to the same authenticated workspace.
