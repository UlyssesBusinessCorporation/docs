Welcome to Ulysses docs.

Ulysses helps public-sector and mission-driven teams understand funding, manage programs, and move work forward with less spreadsheet friction. This site is where we publish product guides, concepts, and how-to pages as they land.

If you are just getting started, begin with the [Quickstart](/quickstart). For longer-form how-tos, see [Guides](/guides/overview).
