This quickstart takes you from account access to a first productive session in the Ulysses app.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, you need:

- An invitation or account for your organization
- Access to [app.tryulysses.com](https://app.tryulysses.com) in a modern browser

## Get started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to [https://app.tryulysses.com](https://app.tryulysses.com) and sign in with the identity provider your organization uses.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your workspace">
    After sign-in, confirm you are in the correct organization workspace. If you belong to more than one, switch to the workspace you intend to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore your home surface">
    From the main app, open the areas your role uses most—projects, funding, or chat with Ulysses—and complete one concrete task (for example, open a project or ask a question about your data).
  </Step>
</Steps>
