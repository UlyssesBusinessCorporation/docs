This page lists what Ulysses has shipped and what is in development, newest quarter first. Features from 2026 Q3 are available now. Later quarters are targets and can change.

## 2027 Q2

- **MCP access.** Connect AI agents to your workspace through the Model Context Protocol. See [MCP](/interfaces/mcp).

## 2027 Q1

- **Audits and public comments.** Keep the records you need to respond to audits and public comments. See [Respond to audits and public comments](/guides/respond-to-audits).
- **Reporting.** Report results, use of funds, and project and program status to your board of supervisors, city council, and the public. See [Report with confidence](/guides/report-with-confidence).
- **Automated invoice generation.**
- **Agent workflows.**

## 2026 Q4

- **Collaborative applications.** Draft and review NOFO applications with your team in one workspace. See [Pursue grants collaboratively](/guides/pursue-grants).
- **Compliance reporting.** Track reporting requirements after award.

## 2026 Q3

- **Funding portfolio.** See all your projects and programs in one place, with each one's funding status and capital stack. See [See all your projects and programs](/guides/see-your-portfolio).
- **Opportunity matching.** Match federal and state NOFOs to your projects and programs, with a score and the reasons behind each match. See [Match opportunities](/guides/match-opportunities).
- **Award tracking.** Track award lifecycles, including obligations, drawdowns, reimbursements, and clawback warnings. See [Manage the funding lifecycle](/guides/manage-funding-lifecycle).
- **Connections and datasets.** Connect external spreadsheets and sync them into projects, programs, and awards. See [Bring in your spreadsheets](/guides/bring-in-spreadsheets).

For what's available today, see the [Introduction](/introduction).
